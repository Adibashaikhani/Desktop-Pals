How to use Desktop Pals

  1. Install and open it

    Get Desktop Pals from the Microsoft Store and open it from the Start menu.

  2. Make it yours

    The customize window opens the first time. Choose a pet, a color, an outfit, and a size. Type in the “Try it out” box to see it react.

  3. Close the window and get to work

    Your pet stays in the corner of your screen and reacts as you type in any app.

  4. Start a focus session

    Right-click the tray icon (the small green dot near the clock, possibly under the ^ arrow) and choose “Start focus session”, or use the Focus tab.

Private by design

To react as you type, Desktop Pals counts key presses. It never reads which keys you press, never records what you type, and never sends anything over the internet. There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Your choices are saved on your own PC.

Questions

Is Desktop Pals free?

Yes. There are no ads, accounts, or in-app purchases.

Does it record what I type?

No. It only counts that a key was pressed so your pet can animate. It cannot tell which key it was, and nothing about your typing is stored or sent anywhere.

Why does it need to watch my keyboard?

So your pet can react when you type in any app, not just its own window. The listener only counts presses.

Will it block my clicks?

No. Clicks pass straight through to the apps underneath. Only the pet itself can be clicked or dragged.

Where is the settings window, and how do I quit?

Double-click your pet, or find the small green dot in the system tray near the clock (it may be under the ^ arrow). Right-click it for the menu, which includes Quit.

Does it work with more than one monitor?

Yes. Drag it to any screen. It stays on screen and above that screen’s taskbar.

It appears over my full-screen game or video. What can I do?

Your pet floats above other windows. Right-click the tray icon and untick “Show pet” while you play or watch, then tick it again afterwards.

Can I turn off the wandering, speech or reactions?

Yes. The Behavior tab lets you switch off idle antics, strolling, speech bubbles and gentle reactions. Sounds are off until you turn them on.

What do I need to run it?

A PC running Windows 10 or 11.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. Desktop Pals is Windows only for now.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find Desktop Pals, and choose Uninstall.
