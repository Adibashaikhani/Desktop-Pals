Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Desktop Pals is a desktop pet for Windows. This page explains exactly what it does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing.

What the app listens to

So your pet can react when you type or click in any app, Desktop Pals uses a system-wide input listener. It only counts that a key was pressed or the mouse was clicked. It does not read which key was pressed, does not record or store what you type, and does not log the apps you use.

What is stored on your PC

  • Your choices: pet, color, size, name, accessories, season and behavior options
  • Where you left your pet on screen
  • How many focus sessions you finished today, and the dates of the last morning greeting and late-night nudge (so they appear once)

This is saved in a small settings file on your own computer and is removed when you uninstall the app.

What is sent over the internet

Nothing. Desktop Pals has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests.

Other things the app may do

  • Battery: on laptops, it reads the battery level on your device to show a low-battery message. This stays on your PC.
  • Clipboard and images: the app only writes to the clipboard, or saves a file, when you click Copy sticker or Save. It never reads your clipboard.
  • Sounds: off by default. They are generated in the app and use no recordings.

Children

Desktop Pals does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
